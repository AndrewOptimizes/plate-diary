App design concept

Remember every meal, one photo at a time.

Plate Diary is a food journal that starts with a photo. Snap your plate, add a few words, and your meals become a visual diary you'll actually keep.

01 · The problem

Food journals are tedious. Photos are scattered.

Written food logs ask for too much: portions, ingredients, times. Most people give up within a week. Meanwhile, they already photograph their food, but those photos get buried between screenshots and receipts in the camera roll.

Design goal: make logging a meal take under ten seconds, and make looking back feel rewarding instead of clinical.

02 · What people want

Three main end goals

🧘

Mindful habits

See when, what and how you eat on a visual timeline, so triggers and habits stand out without weighing or counting anything.

📝

Symptoms, mood & context

Note who you ate with, where, and how you felt, slept or stressed, to connect meals with discomfort, sensitivities or emotional eating.

📖

Memories & keepsakes

A guilt-free diary of favorite dishes, new restaurants and shared meals: more scrapbook than diet log.

03 · Key features

Small, focused, fast

📸

Snap & caption

One photo and one line of text. Everything else is optional.

🏷️

Meal tags

Breakfast, lunch, dinner or snack in one tap, then filter the feed by any of them.

🧭

Optional context

Mood, company, place and how you felt after, one tap each, tucked away until you want it.

⭐

Quick rating

Five stars to remember how much you loved it.

📅

Calendar view

See your month as a grid of plates and spot patterns at a glance.

🔒

Private by default

Your diary stays on your device. No feed, no followers, no pressure.

04 · User flow

From plate to diary in five steps

  1. 1Open the app
  2. 2Snap or upload a photo
  3. 3Add a caption, tag & optional context
  4. 4Save
  5. 5Browse feed or calendar
